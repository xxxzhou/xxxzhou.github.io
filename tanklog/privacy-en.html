TankLog Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

TankLog is a local-first aquarium care log provided by Xin Zhou. We keep your data out of our hands: there is no account, no analytics, and no advertising.

This policy applies to all features of the app. By using TankLog you acknowledge this policy.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not upload personal data to the developer or to any third-party server.

Where your data lives

  • Tanks, water tests, maintenance logs, livestock, plants, equipment, tasks, and photos are stored on your device inside the app sandbox.
  • If you are signed in to iCloud and iCloud Drive is available, the app writes a copy to your own iCloud account. The developer cannot access it.
  • When you export CSV, PDF, or a JSON backup, the file goes wherever you choose to save or share it.

Permissions

  • Notifications: used for local reminders such as water changes, dosing, and equipment maintenance. Declining notifications does not affect the rest of the app.
  • Photos: the app only reads the photos you explicitly pick with the system photo picker. It does not scan your library and does not upload photos.

Purchases

TankLog Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple. Payment details are handled by Apple; the developer does not receive your card or billing information.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or social SDKs.

Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect any age or identity information.

Your choices

  • Delete all local records and photos in Settings → Clear All Data.
  • Turn notifications off in system Settings.
  • Uninstalling the app removes local data. Copies in your iCloud Drive can be deleted from the Files app or iCloud settings.

Disclaimer

Water parameter ranges, CO2 estimates, and fertilizer calculations are reference values only. They are not professional husbandry or medical advice.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
