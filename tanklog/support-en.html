TankLog Support

TankLog is a local-first aquarium care log for planted tanks, shrimp tanks, reef tanks, and multi-tank keepers. If you need help, have a suggestion, or want to recover data, contact us using the details below.

FAQ

Where is my data stored?

Tanks, water tests, maintenance logs, livestock, plants, equipment, tasks, and photos are stored on your device inside the app sandbox. If you are signed in to iCloud with iCloud Drive enabled, a copy is also written to your own iCloud account. The developer cannot access it.

How do I move to a new phone?

Sign in to the same iCloud account on both devices with iCloud Drive enabled. Alternatively, use Settings → Back Up All Data to export JSON, transfer it with AirDrop or the Files app, then use Restore from Backup on the new phone. The JSON backup contains records and photo references, not the photo files themselves; photos sync through iCloud or can be imported again.

Why am I not receiving reminders?

Open iOS Settings → Notifications → TankLog and allow notifications. The app's Settings page shows the current authorization status. Completing a repeating task schedules the next reminder automatically.

Are the water ranges and fertilizer calculations accurate?

Water parameter ranges, CO2 estimates, and post-water-change dosing are reference values only. They are not professional husbandry or medical advice. Fertilizer concentrations vary by brand; start with a reduced dose and observe for 2–3 days.

I bought Pro. How do I restore it on a new phone?

Use the same Apple ID and tap Restore Purchases in Settings. Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Clear All Data removes all local records and photos. Copies in iCloud Drive must be deleted from the Files app or iCloud settings. Uninstalling the app also removes local data.

Are photos compressed?

Yes. Imported photos are resized to a maximum dimension of 1600 px and stored as JPEG.

Can I use the fertilizer calculator for a reef tank?

The calculator is intended for planted and shrimp tanks. Reef tanks normally do not use this dosing approach; the app shows a note on that page.
