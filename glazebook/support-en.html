GlazeBook Support

GlazeBook is a local-first record-keeping app for pottery studios: glaze recipes, test tile comparison, piece tracking, kiln logs, and recipe card PDFs. For questions or feedback, reach us any of the ways below.

FAQ

Where is my data stored?

Everything — recipes, test tiles, pieces, kiln runs, and photos — stays on your device inside the app sandbox. The developer has no access. There are no accounts and no cloud sync.

How do I move to a new phone?

  1. On the old phone: Settings → Export Full Backup (ZIP, includes photos), then transfer the file (AirDrop or Files).
  2. On the new phone: install GlazeBook, Settings → Import Backup, pick the ZIP, and confirm the overwrite.
  3. Legacy JSON backups (without photos) are supported too.

What is "Local Backups"?

Whenever the app goes to the background it keeps a rolling local backup — one every 12 hours, the most recent 14. Tap any entry under Settings → Local Backups to restore it, so accidental deletions are recoverable.

What are the free limits?

The free version allows 5 glazes, 20 pieces, and 3 kiln runs. Editing existing entries never counts against the limit. Unlock Pro (one-time purchase) for unlimited. Backup export and recipe card PDFs are free forever.

I bought Pro — how do I restore it on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple ID and use Settings → Unlock Pro → Restore Purchases. Pro is a one-time buy, not a subscription.

What are the images at the front of each list?

The glaze list shows the cover photo of the latest test tile; pieces prefer their own photo, falling back to their glaze's test tile image; kiln runs show their firing photo. Tap any row to edit, then tap a photo to set it as the cover (marked with a badge).

It says "Cannot connect to the App Store"

Purchasing requires network access and a reachable App Store. If the store works elsewhere but the purchase still fails, make sure an Apple ID is signed in system-wide and retry, or use Restore Purchases.

How do I generate recipe card PDFs?

Settings → Generate Recipe Card PDF renders one page per glaze (with test tile thumbnails and total-percentage checks), ready to share or print for the studio wall.

How do I delete data?

Swipe left on any list row to delete; linked photos are removed with it. Uninstalling clears all local data. Exported backups are yours to manage.
