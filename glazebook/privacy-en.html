GlazeBook Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

GlazeBook is a local-first record-keeping app for pottery studios, provided by individual developer Xin Zhou. We keep your data untouched as much as possible: no accounts, no analytics, no ads.

This policy applies to all features of the app. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not upload any personal data to the developer or to third-party servers.

Where your data lives

  • Glaze recipes, test tiles, pieces, kiln runs, and photos are stored on your device (inside the app sandbox) by default.
  • The app keeps local rolling backups (the most recent 14 by default) and lets you export full backups (ZIP) or JSON backups. These backups stay inside the app sandbox on your device; when you export and share them, you choose where the files go.
  • The developer has no access to any data on your device.

Permissions

  • Camera: used to photograph test tiles, pieces, and kiln firings. If declined, the app still works — you can pick images from your photo library instead.
  • Photos: the app uses the system photo picker and only reads the images you explicitly select. It never scans your library or uploads photos.

In-App Purchase

GlazeBook Pro is a one-time, non-consumable purchase through the Apple App Store. Payment is handled by Apple; the developer never sees your card or billing details.

Third parties

The app integrates no ads, analytics, or third-party SDKs.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no age information.

Your choices

  • Deleting a glaze, test tile, piece, or kiln run in a list also deletes its photos.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all local data and photos.
  • Exported backup files are yours to keep; deleting them is outside the app's control.

Disclaimer

Recipes and firing notes in the app are your personal records only and do not constitute formulation advice. Firing results depend on materials, kiln, and atmosphere — always fire test tiles first.
