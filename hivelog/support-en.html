HiveLog Support

HiveLog is a local-first beekeeping record book for hive profiles, inspections, mite counts, and treatments. If you need help or have a suggestion, contact us below.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

All records and photos are stored on your device only. Use "Settings → 导出完整备份（ZIP，含照片）" to create a full backup, or "导出 JSON 备份" for data only.

How do I move to a new phone?

Export the full backup ZIP on the old phone, transfer it with Files or AirDrop, and keep a copy in iCloud Drive or your computer. HiveLog has no account, so the backup file is what carries your data.

Camera, photos, or notifications are not working

Open system Settings → HiveLog and enable Camera, Photos, or Notifications. Taking photos requires a real device.

Why is the purchase button missing its price?

The price is loaded from the App Store. If it does not appear, check your network or sign in to the App Store with your Apple ID, then reopen the paywall.

Does deleting the app delete my data?

Yes, deleting the app removes local data. Export a full backup ZIP first and keep it somewhere safe.

Is HiveLog a legal record-keeping system?

No. HiveLog is a recording and export tool only. It does not replace statutory record-keeping obligations and does not constitute veterinary advice. Always follow the label and local regulations for bee medicines.
