HiveLog Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

HiveLog is a local-first beekeeping record book (hive profiles, inspection logs, mite counts, and treatment records), provided by independent developer Xin Zhou ("we", "us"). This policy applies to all features of the app. By using HiveLog you agree to this policy.

Summary

  • There is no account system. We do not ask you to register and we do not collect your identity.
  • Hives, inspections, treatments, photos, and exports stay on your device.
  • The app contains no analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs. No data is sent to a developer server.
  • Data leaves your device only when you export it yourself (backup ZIP, JSON, or record PDF).

1. Information We Collect

1.1 What we do not collect

We do not collect, generate, or upload:

  • registration or login information (there is no account system);
  • usage analytics, behavioural data, advertising identifiers (IDFA), or device fingerprints;
  • crash reports or performance monitoring data;
  • your hive records, inspection records, treatment records, or photos;
  • precise location, contacts, microphone, or health data.

1.2 Data stored only on your device

3. Data Sharing

We do not collect data, so we do not sell, share, or use it for advertising. The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

If you use the system share sheet to export a PDF, JSON, or ZIP backup, the recipient is your choice. Data is handled under that recipient's own privacy policy after it leaves your device.

4. Storage and Retention

  • Data is stored in the app sandbox and kept until you delete it.
  • Deleting a hive removes its records and attached photos from the device.
  • Deleting the app removes local data. Files you exported to other locations must be deleted in Files yourself.
  • Exported backups (JSON / ZIP) belong to you; keep them safe.

5. Your Rights

In the app you can:

  • Access and correct hive, inspection, and treatment records.
  • Export a JSON backup, a full backup ZIP including photos, and an apiary record PDF.
  • Delete individual records and photos, or delete the app to erase local data.
  • Withdraw permission for camera, photos, or notifications in system Settings.

If you cannot do this in the app, contact us at the email below.

6. Children's Privacy

HiveLog is not directed to children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect children's personal information. If you believe we hold such data, contact us and we will delete it.

7. International Transfers

The developer does not receive or store your data, so there is no developer-side cross-border transfer.

8. Security

HiveLog relies on the iOS sandbox and data-protection mechanisms. Exported files are not additionally encrypted; keep shared or saved files safe. No system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date. Material changes will be noted in the app or on this page.
