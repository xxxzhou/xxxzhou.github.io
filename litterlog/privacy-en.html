LitterLog Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

LitterLog is a local-first breeding manager for catteries and kennels, provided by independent developer Xin Zhou ("we", "us"). This policy applies to all features of the app. By using LitterLog you agree to this policy.

Summary

  • There is no account system. We do not ask you to register and we do not collect your identity.
  • Cats, litters, kittens, customers, payments, photos, and backups stay on your device.
  • The app contains no analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs. No data is sent to a developer server.
  • Data leaves your device only when you export it yourself or choose an iCloud Drive folder.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 What we do not collect

We do not collect, generate, or upload:

  • registration or login information (there is no account system);
  • usage analytics, behavioural data, advertising identifiers (IDFA), or device fingerprints;
  • crash reports or performance monitoring data;
  • your photos, customer records, or any breeding records;
  • precise location, contacts, microphone, or health data.

You can turn off each permission in system Settings at any time. The corresponding feature will stop working, but existing data is unaffected.

3. Data Sharing

We do not collect data, so we do not sell, share, or use it for advertising. The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

If you use the system share sheet to export a PDF, CSV, or ZIP, the recipient is your choice. Data is handled under that recipient's own privacy policy after it leaves your device.

4. Storage and Retention

  • Data is stored in the app sandbox and kept until you delete it.
  • Automatic backups are created at most once every 12 hours, and the latest 14 are kept. Older backups are removed automatically.
  • You can erase all records and photos in "Me → Erase all data".
  • Deleting the app removes local data. Files you exported to other locations must be deleted in Files yourself.
  • Backups in iCloud Drive belong to your own iCloud account. The developer cannot access them.

5. Your Rights

In the app you can:

  • Access and correct cat, litter, kitten, customer, health, and payment records.
  • Export a full ZIP backup, a cat CSV, litter records, pedigree certificates, and handover PDFs.
  • Delete individual records, photos, and backups, or erase all data in Settings.
  • Withdraw permission for camera, photos, notifications, or file access in system Settings.

If you cannot do this in the app, contact us at the email below.

6. Children's Privacy

LitterLog is not directed to children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect children's personal information. If you believe we hold such data, contact us and we will delete it.

7. International Transfers

The developer does not receive or store your data, so there is no developer-side cross-border transfer. If you choose iCloud Drive, Apple processes the data under its own terms.

8. Security

LitterLog relies on the iOS sandbox and data-protection mechanisms. Exported ZIP files are not additionally encrypted; keep shared or saved files safe. No system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date. Material changes will be noted in the app or on this page.
