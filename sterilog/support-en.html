SteriLog Support

SteriLog is a local-first sterilization and infection-control log for tattoo, piercing, dental and med-spa studios. For questions, feedback, or help restoring data, reach us below.

FAQ

Where is my data stored?

Autoclaves, cycles, spore tests, staff, sharps logs and photos live on your device (app sandbox). Nothing is uploaded to any server.

How do I move to a new phone?

On the old phone: Settings → 生成 ZIP 备份（含照片） (Generate ZIP backup with photos) and share it (AirDrop or Files). On the new phone: Settings → 从 ZIP 备份恢复 (Restore from ZIP backup) and pick the file — all records and photos are restored.

Not receiving reminders?

Enable notifications in iOS Settings → Notifications → SteriLog, then turn on Settings → 提醒通知 in the app. Spore tests and BBP training are reminded at 9:00 AM on their due date.

What if a spore test comes back positive?

The app requires a corrective action, automatically quarantines the autoclave (red warning in lists), and lets you lift the quarantine after corrective actions are done. Follow your local regulations and device manufacturer guidance for actual remediation.

Bought Pro — how do I restore on a new phone?

Use the same Apple ID and tap 恢复购买 (Restore Purchases) on the paywall. SteriLog Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → 清空所有数据 (Erase All Data) removes all local records and photos and resets settings. Export a backup first. Uninstalling also removes local data.

Are photos compressed?

Thumbnails are compressed for lists; originals are kept for export and backup.
