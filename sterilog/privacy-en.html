SteriLog Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

SteriLog (净记) is a local-first sterilization and infection-control log for tattoo, piercing, dental and med-spa studios, provided by independent developer Xin Zhou. We keep your data as private as possible: no account, no analytics, no ads.

This policy applies to all features of the app. By using the app you agree to this policy.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not upload any personal data to the developer or any third-party server.

Where data lives

  • Autoclaves, cycles, spore tests, staff training, sharps incidents and photos are stored on your device (app sandbox) by default.
  • When you export a JSON / ZIP backup / inspection PDF, the file goes wherever you choose; backups contain your records and photos.

Permissions

  • Camera: to capture photos of chemical indicators and spore test vials as record attachments. If denied, you can still pick from your photo library or log manually.
  • Notifications: for local due-date reminders for spore tests and BBP training. If denied, the app still works; you just will not receive reminders.
  • Photo library: only the image you actively pick is read. Your library is never scanned or uploaded.

In-app purchase

SteriLog Pro is a one-time non-consumable purchase through the Apple App Store. Payments are handled by Apple; the developer never sees your card or billing details.

Third parties

The app integrates no ads, analytics, or social SDKs.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no age information.

Your choices

  • Delete all local records, photos and settings via Settings → 清空所有数据 (Erase All Data).
  • Revoke camera or notification permission in iOS Settings.
  • Uninstalling the app removes local data; keep exported backups safe.

Disclaimer

SteriLog is a record-keeping and export tool only. It is not professional medical or legal advice, does not replace statutory record-keeping obligations, does not certify compliance, and does not guarantee inspection outcomes.
