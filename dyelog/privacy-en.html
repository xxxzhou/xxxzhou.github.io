DyeLog Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

DyeLog is a local-first hair color formula archive for hairstylists and colorists, provided by Xin Zhou. We keep your data out of our hands: there is no account, no analytics, and no advertising.

This policy applies to all features of the app. By using DyeLog you acknowledge this policy.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not upload personal data to the developer or to any third-party server.

Where your data lives

  • Client profiles, color formulas, and photos are stored on your device inside the app sandbox.
  • The app keeps rolling backups of your records on your device. Backups stay inside the app sandbox and are excluded from iCloud backups.
  • When you export a color card PDF, a ZIP backup, or a JSON backup, the file goes wherever you choose to save or share it.

Permissions

  • Photos: the app reads only the images you explicitly pick with the system photo picker. It does not scan your library and does not upload photos.
  • Camera: the camera is used only when you take a photo for a client card. Shots are stored inside the app only; nothing is recorded or uploaded outside it.

Purchases

DyeLog Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple. Payment details are handled by Apple; the developer does not receive your card or billing information.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or social SDKs.

Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect any age or identity information.

Your choices

  • Deleting a client removes their formulas and photos as well.
  • Export a full ZIP backup (photos included) anytime from Settings.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all local data.

Disclaimer

DyeLog is a record-keeping tool. Color results depend on hair base and condition; the app does not provide colorimetry advice or guarantee results.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
