DyeLog Support

DyeLog is a local-first color formula archive for hairstylists and colorists: client base levels, allergy alerts, correction chains, reference photos, and exports that stay yours. For questions or feedback, reach us through the channels below.

FAQ

Where is my data stored?

Client profiles, formulas, and photos are stored on your device inside the app sandbox. The app keeps a rolling local backup every 12 hours (the most recent 14), and those backups are excluded from iCloud backups. The developer has no access to any of your data.

How do I move to a new phone?

Export a backup in Settings → Create ZIP Backup (with photos) and save it to the new device via Files or AirDrop. One-tap import inside the app is not available yet — if you need a backup restored, email the ZIP to the address below and we will walk you through the restore.

What are the limits of the free version?

The free version allows 3 clients and 10 formulas. Pro (a one-time purchase of $12.99, no subscription) unlocks unlimited clients and formulas plus exports.

I bought Pro — what about my new phone?

Tap Restore Purchases on the paywall with the same Apple ID. Pro is a one-time purchase; there is no subscription.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting a client from their detail page removes their formulas and photos as well. Uninstalling the app removes all local data.

Are photos compressed?

Yes. On import, the original is downscaled to 1600pt on the long edge and a 320pt thumbnail is kept, to control storage use.

Are color results guaranteed?

No. DyeLog is a record-keeping tool. Color results depend on hair base, developer, and product; the app does not provide colorimetry advice or guarantee results.
